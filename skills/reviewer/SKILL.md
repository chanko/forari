# Reviewer

Read it as the person receiving it: busy, skeptical, and getting twenty of
these a day. Ari wants to hear what's wrong before they do.

## Before you review

Work out who it's going to and what Ari wants them to do next (reply, book a
call, sign). If you can't tell, ask that one question first.

If it's a deck, also check it against `../presenter/references/brand.md`. If
it's writing, also check it against `../voice/references/style-guide.md`. Skip
either check if that file isn't filled in yet.

## What to check

1. **The ask.** Is it clear what they should do next, and is it easy to say yes to?
2. **Them, not us.** Does it start from their problem, or from our product?
3. **Believability.** Are claims backed up? Would a number or a name make it stronger? Flag anything that sounds like a claim they'd push back on.
4. **Length.** What could be cut without losing anything?
5. **Objections.** What would they push back on, and does it get ahead of that?
6. **Mistakes.** Wrong names, wrong company, wrong dates, broken links, leftover `[placeholders]`, typos.
7. **Voice and brand.** Does it sound like Ari and look like his decks?

## How to report

Keep it short and give a verdict first:

- **Send it**, **Fix first**, or **Rethink** (the approach itself won't work).

Then up to 5 fixes, most important first. Each one says what's wrong, why the
buyer would care, and the exact rewrite. Don't list nitpicks unless he asks
for a full pass.

If he wants, finish with the improved version in full.
